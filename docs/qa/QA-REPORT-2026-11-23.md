# Pharmacy QA-1 — 23 November 2026

**Tested release** — `develop` at `78c36461e44f365c432e1ff0686be6c5d45fe20e`. Synthetic database only. All amounts below are in EGP unless marked as piastres.

**Result** — 13 reproduced defects, including five P1 issues. Ten discounted-strip refunds matched the expected 40.50. Backup restore into a fresh synthetic data folder verified successfully.

**Severity** — P0 means an immediate stop due to systemic loss or unsafe dispensing. P1 means a routine workflow risks incorrect money, pricing or stock, or loses a required operation. P2 means a material defect with a workaround or a narrower impact. P3 means a minor presentation or guidance defect. No P0 was reproduced in the exercised cases.

## Reproduced defects

### QA-001 · P1 · Purchase approval gives no choice about repricing existing stock

- **Screen and steps** — On the company purchase screen, choose an item with stock in lots priced at 80.00 and 100.00. Add an incoming later-expiry lot at 170.00, enter the paper total and approve.
- **Expected** — Acknowledge the price increase and ask the owner whether to retain the existing lot prices or apply the new public price to the affected on-hand stock. Make the scope of either choice explicit.
- **Actual** — The draft warns of a public price change. The approval confirmation mentions stock, cost and payment only. Approval writes the new item public price and creates the new lot at its own price. Existing lots retain their previous prices without a decision.
- **Suggested fix** — Present the affected lots, quantities and old/new values during approval. Require an explicit keep/reprice choice for each affected item, record it with the approval, and preserve prices on completed sales.
- **Source** — [Approval confirmation](../../app/web/app.js#L1631), [lot and public price updates](../../app/purchasing/service.py#L867). **Regression** — `test_QA_001_purchase_prompts_about_repricing_stock`.

### QA-002 · P1 · Sale search quotes a different price from the cart

- **Screen and steps** — Search for the synthetic item with an earlier sellable lot at 80.00 and a current catalog price of 100.00. Read the result price and select one box. Repeat before and after approving the higher-price purchase.
- **Expected** — The price quoted for the offered sale unit reflects the lot the cart will allocate. If availability makes the price conditional, show the available prices and quantities before the customer is quoted.
- **Actual** — The browser result displayed 100.00 and the cart charged 80.00. Search displays the item price while allocation uses the lot price. After the higher-price purchases and subsequent stock count, another browser check showed 217.00 in search and 100.00 in the cart.
- **Suggested fix** — Return a quote from the same sellable-stock and expiry-order calculation used by the basket. Show a clear lot-price breakdown when the requested quantity spans multiple prices and revalidate changed quotes at checkout.
- **Source** — [Sale search price](../../app/web/app.js#L4013), [sale allocation pricing](../../app/sales/service.py#L615). **Regression** — `test_QA_002_sale_result_exposes_the_price_it_will_charge`.

### QA-003 · P1 · Manual purchase cannot record a line's VAT

- **Screen and steps** — Create a company purchase. Add a line with amount, percentage discount, distributor margin, bonus and expiry. Try to enter the VAT printed on the supplier's paper in quick entry and in the saved line grid. Repeat on a warehouse purchase.
- **Expected** — Enter and review the line's VAT and its included/additive treatment, then match the stated paper total to the piastre. Preserve the breakdown when approving or returning a line.
- **Actual** — Both manual entry and the line grid offer public price, discount, margin, net and total, but expose zero tax controls. The line model supports `tax_amount`. A cashier entering an invoice with separately stated VAT cannot record the breakdown through this screen.
- **Suggested fix** — Add a clearly labelled VAT input to the manual line and show tax on review. Make inclusive and additive amounts explicit, show their effect on the paper total, and use the existing tax model after agreeing the calculation with the owner.
- **Source** — [Quick purchase form](../../app/web/app.js#L1367), [line grid](../../app/web/app.js#L1480), [VAT handling](../../app/purchasing/validation.py). **Regression** — `test_QA_003_manual_purchase_has_a_vat_field`.

### QA-004 · P1 · A wallet-paid invoice has no refund option for the original wallet

- **Screen and steps** — Sell a box to a walk-in customer using «محفظة إلكترونية». Open the completed sale and inspect «طريقة الاسترداد».
- **Expected** — An eligible, confirmed payment can be refunded to its original method and account up to the received amount still available. A different payout method remains an approved exception.
- **Actual** — The sale completes as a transfer. The walk-in invoice offers only cash on the return screen. The back end groups transfer and card funds into a card cap, but the form offers «بطاقة» only when the invoice has a card payment. The cashier has no same-method path for the wallet.
- **Suggested fix** — Add a distinct transfer/wallet refund path tied to the original payment channel and its verified received amount. Reconcile it in payment-channel, return and shift reports.
- **Source** — [Refund options](../../app/web/app.js#L3159), [allowed return methods](../../app/sales/service.py#L1339), [refund caps](../../app/sales/service.py#L1466). **Regression** — `test_QA_004_guest_wallet_sale_offers_refund_to_original_method`.

### QA-005 · P1 · Sales invoice filters can show stale financial totals

- **Screen and steps** — On the owner sales invoice list, toggle the cash filter on and then immediately off. Delay delivery of the first browser response to reproduce an older response arriving last.
- **Expected** — The list, totals and exports follow the currently selected filters, with older responses discarded.
- **Actual** — With cash visibly off, the page showed 5 cash invoices where the unfiltered response contained 11. The late cash-only response overwrote the current selection's table.
- **Suggested fix** — Increment a request sequence for every load and render only the latest request for that screen. Invalidate the sequence on navigation, and keep export data tied to the visible selection.
- **Source** — [Invoice report loading and rendering](../../app/web/app.js#L2963), [filter handler](../../app/web/app.js#L3075). **Regression** — `test_QA_005_latest_invoice_filter_owns_the_totals`.

### QA-006 · P2 · Completed invoice detail hides gross line prices and discounts

- **Screen and steps** — Sell one 100.00 box plus one 50.00 strip of the same item, add a 200.00 second item, give the first line 10% off and then discount the invoice 10%. Open the completed sale from the invoice list.
- **Expected** — Show quantity, official price of the sold units, line gross, discounts and net. Show the invoice gross, total discount and amount due in that order.
- **Actual** — The full invoice detail has item, quantity, net, lots and return quantity. The amount it calls «الإجمالي» is the 301.50 amount due. The 350.00 gross and 48.50 discount are absent. The browser receipt does show the three totals for this ordinary sale. The owner's inline invoice list shows gross and net, but its line discount column shows only the line's own percentage. On the second line it was blank despite the 20.00 invoice discount share.
- **Suggested fix** — Use the saved sale and allocation snapshot to show gross, line discount, allocated invoice discount and net in invoice detail and list. Use matching labels on screen and print.
- **Source** — [Completed invoice summary](../../app/web/app.js#L3297), [line detail](../../app/web/app.js#L3317), [inline invoice detail](../../app/web/app.js#L3034), [invoice report line fields](../../app/reports/invoices.py#L156).

### QA-007 · P2 · Imported `20TAB` with unknown strip layout looks like a confirmed box-only item

- **Screen and steps** — Import an item CSV with name `QA Unmapped 20TAB`, price 50, and no pack-unit column. Review, apply, search the item and run «فحص بيانات الأصناف».
- **Expected** — Surface the missing dispensing layout as a review decision for products with a tablet/capsule count. Confirm whether the pharmacy sells whole boxes or strips from the physical pack or a trusted source. `20TAB` specifies tablet count. A 20-tablet box can have different strip layouts.
- **Actual** — Preview allows import with a missing recommended units column. The item is stored as pack size 1 and base unit `unit`. Item health does not flag this item and POS shows the strip action only for multi-unit packs. Manual pack correction is available once the owner identifies the item.
- **Suggested fix** — Keep pack provenance and ask for confirmation on ambiguous imported counted products. Offer a review queue and a batch-safe correction link. Keep explicitly confirmed whole-box items valid.
- **Source** — [Import units and recommended columns](../../app/dataimport/service.py#L93), [normalization of blank pack cells](../../app/dataimport/service.py#L513), [whole-box exception in item health](../../app/reports/item_health.py#L49), [POS unit buttons](../../app/web/app.js#L4015). Verified for the generic CSV flow. The equivalent live legacy export shape is outside this reproduction.

### QA-008 · P2 · Warehouse quick entry still offers company-style bonus

- **Screen and steps** — Open manual drafts for suppliers explicitly typed Company and Warehouse. Compare the quick-entry forms.
- **Expected** — Show the bonus input for company purchasing and hide it by default in warehouse quick entry, per the owner's purchase-screen specification.
- **Actual** — Both drafts showed the bonus control. The payment default did change correctly from credit for the company to drawer for the warehouse.
- **Suggested fix** — Make the quick-entry inputs reflect supplier type, including updates after a supplier change. Preserve accurate display of any bonus on a previously saved invoice.
- **Source** — [Quick entry bonus field](../../app/web/app.js#L1372), [payment defaults](../../app/purchasing/service.py#L366), [owner purchase-screen requirements](../HANDOFF.md).

### QA-009 · P2 · A slow previous screen can replace the current screen

- **Screen and steps** — From Users, open a cashier's permissions and immediately navigate to Items before the permissions load. Reproduce by delaying the cashier detail request for 750 ms in Playwright.
- **Expected** — Items remains on screen after the navigation and its controls match the active route.
- **Actual** — The item search first appeared. Then the previous user's permissions table replaced it while the address still showed `#/items`. During the larger route pass, late procurement, settings and item-health responses also logged missing DOM targets after we left those screens.
- **Suggested fix** — Abort pending reads on route changes or validate a navigation token before async render or DOM access. Add browser coverage for common transitions from slow report and settings screens to POS.
- **Source** — [Async route dispatch](../../app/web/app.js#L482), [user detail load and render](../../app/web/app.js#L6770).

### QA-010 · P2 · A shift can require a false discrepancy for an amount the count form cannot represent

- **Screen and steps** — With cash rounding off sell and return amounts that leave an expected drawer amount of 1,411.10. Open shift closing and enter the cash using the offered denominations.
- **Expected** — Provide a supported way to close exact cash or to account explicitly for an uncollectable coin residual, separately from an unexplained shortage.
- **Actual** — The smallest denomination is 0.25. The closest count below the expected amount was 1,411.00. Closing reported a 0.10 shortage even though the residual was expected from the amount-denomination mismatch.
- **Suggested fix** — Make the cash rounding setting compatible with supported denominations, or let the owner classify a controlled residual explicitly. Preserve denomination counts and the audit record.
- **Source** — [Denominations](../../app/web/app.js#L4712), [close payload and discrepancy UI](../../app/web/app.js#L4850). Related to the existing odd-piastres reminder in [handoff](../HANDOFF.md).

### QA-011 · P2 · Receipt omits official prices when a line spans different-price lots

- **Screen and steps** — Stock one box at 80.00 and another box of the same item at 100.00 in a later expiry lot. Sell both in one line and reprint the receipt.
- **Expected** — Show the two quantities and their official prices so the customer can reconcile the line total.
- **Actual** — The receipt shows two boxes and the correct 180.00 total, with neither lot price. Both receipt renderers use the same pricing helper and skip a unit price when it detects more than one lot price.
- **Suggested fix** — Print subrows grouped by saved allocation price and sold unit; keep the shared line totals and receipt summaries intact. For a box plus loose strips, label box and strip prices distinctly.
- **Source** — [Receipt price helper](../../app/printing/escpos.py#L341), [thermal output](../../app/printing/escpos.py#L402), [browser output](../../app/api/app.py#L2882).


### QA-012 · P2 · Manual purchase line entry stalls on a large catalog

- **Screen and steps** — Open a company or warehouse draft, scan a known item and add a line with quantity, price, discount, margin and expiry. Repeat after approving a previous invoice.
- **Expected** — The line and next-entry focus are ready within 300 ms during routine entry.
- **Actual** — Line adds took up to 1.51 s for company and 3.18 s for warehouse. The server logged a 2.83 s purchase-line POST. The cashier has to wait before scanning or typing the next paper line.
- **Suggested fix** — Profile known-item retrieval, match cache invalidation and full draft validation separately. A line with an explicit item ID should avoid irrelevant catalog candidate work while preserving validation and rollback. Re-run the browser timing with a large catalog after each change.
- **Source** — [Quick add UI and focus](../../app/web/app.js#L1413), [line creation and matching](../../app/purchasing/service.py#L615), [catalog matching](../../app/catalog/matching.py#L232).


### QA-013 · P2 · Purchase search filters rows but leaves the previous total

- **Screen and steps** — Open the purchase list for the month with multiple suppliers. Type `QA Synthetic Warehouse` in the text search.
- **Expected** — The footer and export reflect the visible filter, or explicitly identify a whole-period total and whole-period export.
- **Actual** — The table went from 93 rows to 10 warehouse rows. The footer stayed at 92 approved invoices and 323,572.35. The export builder also uses the underlying unfiltered array.
- **Suggested fix** — Share the visibly filtered rows with the footer and export. If a whole-period total is useful, give it a separate explicit label.
- **Source** — [Purchase list filtering and total](../../app/web/app.js#L1135), [purchase workbook rows](../../app/web/app.js#L1175).


## Test setup and scope

- **Data** — The repository’s [synthetic load generator](../../scripts/perf/loadgen.py), seed 253, created 30,000 catalog items and two years of sales from 23 November 2024 through 22 November 2026 in Cairo time. The seed contains 17,662 completed sales, 62 voided sales, 47,157 sale lines, 423 returns, 19,186 batches and 2,499 purchase invoices. The 600 synthetic customers generated 313 customer ledger entries. The sales and purchases exercised a 1,800-item stocked assortment within the full searchable catalog. The selected rate was 22–28 attempts per day. It represents a low-volume till over two years. The fixture therefore does not establish timings for a very busy branch.
- **Fixtures** — The browser scenarios used two-strip boxes, reference items with 80.00, 100.00 and higher public prices on lots with different expiries, known barcodes and internal item codes, a credit customer, a company and a warehouse. Historical seed writes used real services with generator-only indexes and fuzzy advice bypassed for explicit synthetic choices. The snapshot drops the generator indexes. The browser server used unchanged application code and normal matching. A separate small synthetic E-Stock archive supplied legacy routes.
- **Environment** — Linux cloud container, 2 CPU quota, 8 GiB memory limit, Python 3.12.14 for the main run, pinned Node Playwright 1.63.0, with headless system Chromium 154.0.8037.57 because the pinned Chromium download returned 403. Browser locale and time zone were Arabic (Egypt) and Africa/Cairo. A test suite was active during part of the run. Numbers are local observations, with possible CPU contention, and need an isolated repeat before comparing release performance.
- **Limits** — Browser scanner input was simulated through the search field. Browser and ESC/POS content were checked without printer, drawer, barcode scanner or Windows driver hardware. The signed update endpoint returned an environment network 403 during the UI check. Download, install, reboot and Windows rollback were outside the verified browser run. Empty, unauthenticated and main app states were exercised in separate synthetic data folders.

## Automated verification

- The browser regressions in [test_qa_2026_11_23.py](../../tests/test_qa_2026_11_23.py) use `unittest.expectedFailure` for QA-001 through QA-005. Each failed at its behavior assertion when the marker was removed for a diagnostic run.
- Targeted Python 3.13 run (Playwright plus system Chromium) completed 31 tests with 26 passing and five expected failures. It included the QA regressions, help-route checks, receipt parity, update panel, legacy history and stock reconciliation.
- The baseline full suite on Python 3.12 ran 2,978 tests. It recorded 2,941 passes, eight skips, two pre-existing expected failures and 27 failures in the licensing and release-signing fixtures. The key-file guard treated the managed environment’s scratch roots as Git repositories and refused the synthetic fixture paths. Reruns of the 59 affected-module tests on Python 3.13 reproduced the same 27 failures under both `/tmp` and `/var/tmp`. The full-suite gate remains unverified in this environment.

## Development suggestions

Effort is relative to the current codebase. S is a contained change, M is a cross-screen change, and L is a multi-phase integration. Competitor examples come from the repository's market research and are starting points for product comparison.

| What to build | Why it helps a pharmacy | Effort | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Publish a cashier latency budget and run a seeded browser benchmark before releases | Makes the 300 ms goal measurable for scanning, adding and saving during real queues | M | High |
| Give POS a compact cashier layout with a fixed search box, cart directly below, keyboard hints and a one-row collapsible quick-sale strip | Reduces eye and pointer movement during repeated sales and keeps the price and count visible when the basket grows | M | High |
| Add a payment reconciliation queue that groups confirmed, partial, unmatched and refunded wallet/card payments by channel | Helps the owner match deposits and detect collection or handover mistakes without searching invoices individually | L | High |
| Add searchable invoice paging with independent totals for the full filtered period and a consistent export | Gives the owner accurate totals and useful drill-down once a period exceeds the displayed 3,000-invoice cap | M | High |
| Add a supplier entry profile with field order, keyboard sequence, tax and margin basis, preferred payment and remembered item mapping | Cuts retyping and makes company versus warehouse papers easier to reconcile | M | High |
| Offer a verified, optional medicine catalog with sourced barcode, presentation and pack layouts, following the ready-to-use catalog pattern in Medical Group, PharmaCare and PharmaUp | Speeds setup and avoids guessing strip counts. Local owner review can preserve supplier and branch differences | L | Medium |
| Add an encrypted off-device backup option with a guided restore drill to a separate folder | Makes a hardware failure or accidental overwrite recoverable and gives the owner a recent restore result | M | High |
